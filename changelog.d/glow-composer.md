### Added

- Settings has an Appearance page. It holds the theme cards, the main color, the chat wallpaper and the
  glow of the message box. The theme, transparent window and chat background rows moved there from
  General.
- Six new themes: Midnight, Deep Teal, Abyss, Slate, Forest and Sky. A theme card changes the whole app
  at once.
- Main color: pick a preset or any color. It replaces the accent color of every theme.
- Chat wallpaper: Glow, Glyphs, Dots or your own photo, behind the messages of a chat. Strength and blur
  sliders, and a frosted glass switch that makes the bubbles and the message box see-through. The photo
  stays on this computer.
- Message box glow colors: keep the main color, or pick one of six gradients (Sunset, Ocean, Aurora, Neon,
  Fire, Mint). The two lights and the halo take the two colors of the gradient.
- On macOS, a Transparency slider under Transparent window sets how much of the desktop shows through.

### Changed

- The message box has a turning light on its border and a soft halo in the main color. Choose Turn, Still
  or Off. Typing makes the light brighter. With reduced motion, the light does not move.
- Send and your own messages use the main color.

### Fixed

- The Strength and Blur sliders of the chat wallpaper now save when you let go. Before, the chat went back
  to the old value when you left Settings. The memory and CPU limit sliders of a machine had the same fault.
